      EduLudo quick guide

      How to play

      Roll the dice, then click one of your glowing pieces. A piece leaves its castle only on a six. Land on a single opponent to send it home; a safe square protects its pieces. Reach the finish with an exact roll.

      • ? Answer a software engineering question. Correct answers move forward; incorrect answers move back.
      • ! Trigger a surprise: a jump, a setback, or an extra roll.
      • A six earns another roll. Three sixes in a row cancel the third roll.
      • Two same-colour pieces together make a block opponents cannot cross.
