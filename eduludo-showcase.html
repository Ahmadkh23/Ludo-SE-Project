    The quick guide

    How to play

    Roll the dice and move one of your glowing tokens. A six brings a token out of its castle. Be the first to guide all four tokens into your home column.

    • ★ Safe spaces protect tokens from being captured.
    • ? Question spaces test your software engineering knowledge. Correct answers move forward; wrong answers move back.
    • ! Surprise spaces can send you forward, push rivals back, or bring other twists.
    • Land on a rival to send their token back to the castle. A six grants another roll.
    • Boost dice multiply your move; UltraBoost brings bigger risks and bigger leaps.

    Made for curious minds

    About EduLudo

    EduLudo is a university software engineering project exploring how familiar board-game mechanics can make course concepts more engaging. This single-file prototype runs in your browser and keeps its question deck and match history on your device.
